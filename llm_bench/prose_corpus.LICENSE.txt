prose_corpus.txt.gz: the default --shape-mix-corpus for llm_bench

The corpus is the text of three novels, each starting with its own title line:

- Pride and Prejudice, by Jane Austen (first published 1813)
- The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes, by Arthur Conan Doyle (first published 1892)
- Frankenstein, by Mary Shelley (first published 1818)

All three works are in the public domain in the United States and in countries whose copyright terms are the
author's life plus 70 years or less. The text carries no third-party license headers or trademarks. llm_bench uses
it as load-test prompt material: prefixes and tails are cut from it.
